Brazil CADE deadline Monday. Epic says alt distribution in Brazil is 9 steps — not a new US rate.

2026-08-24 · Fortune Insight, LLC · Operator intel for indie iOS teams. This page covers the Brazil storefront and Epic/CADE process. It is not a new US rate sheet — if you only sell US IAP inside the App Store, Connect rules did not change because of this news.

What happened

Brazil's antitrust agency CADE required Apple to respond by Monday 2026-08-24 to Epic's complaint. Epic publicly claims that installing an app through alternative distribution in Brazil takes 9 steps, while the EU path is about 6 steps — that is Epic's narrative, not an official Connect walkthrough.

Meanwhile, Apple's Brazil developer support pages still state that digital goods transactions completed through alternative app marketplaces or web distribution are subject to 5% CTC (Commission on Transactions Completed). That is not the same worksheet as the US Epic link-out proposal in Issue 2. Do not paste Brazil CTC numbers into US SKU math.
