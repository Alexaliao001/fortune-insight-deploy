AutoFill engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Apple's Password AutoFill workflow covers storefront language. Xcode entitlements, extension targets, and Connect actually separate fill behavior from product-page copy:

  • Password AutoFill extension — credential provider target in Xcode. Controls whether your vault can offer saved logins to Safari and apps. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • ASCredentialIdentityStore — publishes credential identities the system can surface in QuickType and AutoFill pickers. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains one-tap fill to buyers.
  • iOS Passwords app integration — users may browse saved logins in Apple's Passwords app when your extension is enabled. Listing copy may reference that workflow; wiring the extension is engineering work, not Connect metadata.
  • Keychain & vault sync — where encrypted passwords live and how they sync. Your listing may mention sync across iPhone and iPad; configuring storage is engineering — AppLocale does not touch it.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of AutoFill capability flags.

A working AutoFill extension and a localized China listing are both required when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other.
