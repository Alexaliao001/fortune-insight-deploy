FAQ

What is Apple Transparency Mode and why does it matter for App Store listing copy?

Transparency Mode is an AirPods listening mode the user selects manually — via Control Center, Settings → Bluetooth → AirPods, or a stem press — that lets outside ambient sound pass through so the wearer hears surroundings while keeping buds in. Apple markets 通透模式 in Simplified Chinese. English marketing on your en-US page does not auto-fill the zh-Hans row in App Store Connect.

How is Transparency Mode listing copy different from Adaptive Audio?

Transparency Mode is the manual passthrough toggle — 通透模式. Adaptive Audio automatically blends ANC and Transparency based on environment — 自适应音频. If your H1 is manual mode switching, lead with Transparency Mode; if auto-blending is your lead story, see our Adaptive Audio listing guide instead.

How is Transparency Mode listing copy different from Spatial Audio or Live Listen?

Spatial Audio is immersive Dolby Atmos or head-tracked 3D playback — 空间音频. Live Listen streams iPhone microphone audio to AirPods for conversation amplification — 实时收听. Does localizing my listing change Transparency Mode behavior on AirPods?
