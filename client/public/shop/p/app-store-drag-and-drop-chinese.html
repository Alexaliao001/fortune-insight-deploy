Drag-and-drop engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume iPad drag-and-drop support covers storefront language. Xcode, entitlements, and Connect actually separate drag gesture behavior from product-page copy:

  • UIDragInteraction & UIDropInteraction — in-app code that enables drag sources and drop targets. Controls which items lift, which views accept drops, and how previews render. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • NSItemProvider & drag payloads — engineering work for cross-app content transfer. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains drag value to buyers.
  • Drag to reorder in lists — UITableView, UICollectionView, or SwiftUI onMove reordering in code. Not App Store Connect listing fields.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of drag-and-drop integration.

Working drag-and-drop flows in the binary and a localized China listing are both required when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other. AppLocale covers how you describe drag-and-drop on the product page in zh-Hans, not how you implement drag interactions or drop zones.
