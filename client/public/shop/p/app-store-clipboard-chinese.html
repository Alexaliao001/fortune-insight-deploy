Clipboard engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume clipboard integration covers storefront language. Xcode, entitlements, and Connect actually separate pasteboard behavior from product-page copy:

  • UIPasteboard & NSPasteboard — in-app code that reads and writes clipboard payloads. Controls which content types copy and paste. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • UIPasteConfiguration & UIPasteControl — engineering work for paste menus and copy controls in SwiftUI or UIKit. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains clipboard value to buyers.
  • Universal Clipboard (Continuity) — system feature for cross-device paste when enabled on signed-in Apple devices. Your listing may mention it; implementing it is not App Store Connect listing fields.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of clipboard integration.

Working copy-and-paste flows in the binary and a localized China listing are both required when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other. AppLocale covers how you describe clipboard workflows on the product page in zh-Hans, not how you implement UIPasteboard or paste controls.
