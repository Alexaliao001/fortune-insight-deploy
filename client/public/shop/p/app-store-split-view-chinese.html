Teams assume iPad Split View support covers storefront language. Xcode, entitlements, and Connect actually separate in-app multitasking behavior from product-page copy:

  • iPad multitasking entitlements & size classes — scene support, window scenes, and layout rules that determine whether your app appears in Split View or Slide Over. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains multitasking value to buyers.
