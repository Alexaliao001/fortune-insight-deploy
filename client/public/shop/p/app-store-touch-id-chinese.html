Touch ID engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Apple's Touch ID workflow covers storefront language. Xcode, entitlements, and Connect actually separate fingerprint unlock behavior from product-page copy:

  • Touch ID capability — toggled in Xcode for apps that authenticate with biometrics. Controls whether the app can call LocalAuthentication. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • LocalAuthentication / LAContext — Swift APIs that present the system fingerprint sheet. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains instant unlock to buyers.
  • canEvaluatePolicy & biometryType — runtime checks for Touch ID vs Face ID availability. Your listing may mention both; detecting device class is engineering work, not listing copy.
  • Secure Enclave & Keychain — where credentials are stored after a successful fingerprint scan. Configuring storage is engineering — AppLocale does not touch it.
  • Privacy nutrition labels — App Store Connect privacy questionnaire when biometrics touch user data. Filing accurate answers is compliance work — AppLocale does not complete it for you.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of Touch ID flags in the binary.

A working Touch ID integration and a localized China listing are both required when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other.
