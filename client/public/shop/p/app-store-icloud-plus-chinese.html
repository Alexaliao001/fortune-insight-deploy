iCloud+ subscription vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Apple's iCloud+ subscription workflow covers storefront language. Settings and App Store Connect actually separate buyer subscription behavior from product-page copy:

  • Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage / Upgrade to iCloud+ — the system screen where buyers choose 50GB, 200GB, or 2TB storage plans and subscribe to iCloud+. Buyers manage this on their devices; it does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Family sharing of iCloud storage — iCloud+ subscribers can share their storage plan with Family Sharing members. Your listing may show this benefit on screenshots; describing buyer-visible family storage sharing is listing copy, not engineering work.
  • iCloud+ bundle inclusions — paid iCloud+ plans include Private Relay, Hide My Email, Custom Email Domain, and HomeKit Secure Video support among other benefits. Listing copy can mention these as plan inclusions when your English page does — not as separate feature listings on this page.
  • Storage tier marketing — 50GB, 200GB, and 2TB plan cards, upgrade prompts when storage is full, and iCloud 储存空间 meters. Description and caption fields explain subscription value; managing buyer Apple subscriptions is outside AppLocale.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of iCloud+ being subscribed on their iPhone.
