China storefront vs US storefront

App Store Connect treats each locale as a separate row. Your en-US listing does not automatically appear on the China storefront — you add a Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans) localization and fill Chinese fields independently. Storefront availability (which countries can download) is separate from language: confirm China is checked under Pricing and Availability before you polish zh-Hans copy. US buyers see en-US; China buyers see zh-Hans when both rows are filled. If zh-Hans is empty, Apple may fall back to English or show a mixed page — neither converts well for Chinese users.

zh-Hans subtitle — 30 characters

The subtitle sits under your app name on the product page and in Search results. Apple’s limit is commonly 30 characters for zh-Hans — verify in Connect before you save. Good subtitles add one fact the title does not repeat: audience (“独立开发者”), workflow (“离线 PDF 批注”), or pricing model (“免费 · 内购解锁”). Bad subtitles mirror English keyword stacks, paste pinyin-only names, or use brochure commas (“智能 · 高效 · 专业”). Write for a Chinese skim reader, not a literal en-US translation.

Keywords field for China

The keyword field is hidden from buyers but indexed for Search on the China storefront. Comma-separated, no spaces after commas (check Connect’s note). Include:

  • Chinese search terms users actually type — problem phrases, category nouns, common misspellings
  • English loanwords Chinese users search locally when relevant (e.g. “Pomodoro”, “PDF”)
  • Synonyms not already visible in title or subtitle — do not duplicate visible copy

Skip competitor names you do not own, trending words your screens do not deliver, and blocks copied unchanged from your en-US keyword row. zh-Hans and en-US keyword fields are independent — US terms rarely map one-to-one to China Search.

Description for Chinese users

Chinese App Store descriptions are skimmed top-down like US listings, but tone and structure differ. Lead with who the app is for and what outcome they get in the first two lines — not a feature bullet dump from your settings menu. Avoid:

  • Formal false cognates copied from English (“专业级”, “强大”) that read as template copy
  • Superlatives without substantiation (“最好”, “#1”, download counts, press quotes from US pages)
  • Mixed English sentences in zh-Hans while screenshots stay untranslated
  • Word-for-word translation of en-US marketing slogans that do not land in Chinese

Better zh-Hans copy reduces bounce on the product page — it does not replace in-app UI localization or Apple’s review decisions.
