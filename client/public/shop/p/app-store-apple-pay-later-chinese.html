Apple Pay Later–specific listing copy — what translates differently

Apple Pay Later listings use vocabulary general utility guides rarely cover. zh-Hans copy should reflect how Chinese App Store readers describe buy-now-pay-later checkout — not a literal paste of US marketing:

US pages use "Apple Pay Later" or "Buy now, pay later." China storefront copy often uses 先买后付 or 先用后付 alongside the product name Apple Pay Later where appropriate on screenshot overlays — especially on checkout and BNPL selection frames under Wallet & Apple Pay.

Pay in 4 & 分四期 / 五周四期

Pay-in-4 over six weeks appears frequently on Apple Pay Later screenshots. Chinese buyers expect 分四期 or 五周四期 for four-payment-over-six-weeks frames — not English "Pay in 4" badges that hide what the app actually markets.

US pages use "No interest and no fees if paid on time" on BNPL value-prop frames. Chinese App Store copy commonly uses 无利息 when that matches your English source — describing the installment terms your gallery shows, not inventing loan terms we do not deliver.
