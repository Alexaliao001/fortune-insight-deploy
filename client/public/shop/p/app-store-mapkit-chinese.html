What good zh-Hans map listing copy looks like

Listing fields should name the map surfaces you actually show in screenshots — aligned with your gallery order:

  • Subtitle — one concrete promise: "地图导航与附近地点发现" or "一键查看路线与周边推荐" — not vague "智能出行"
  • Keywords — mix category terms (地图, 导航, 附近, 路线) with your app's niche within Connect's ~100-character zh-Hans budget; see Simplified Chinese keywords guide
  • Description — short paragraphs for map browse, directions views, nearby lists, saved places, and pin search — mirror screenshot sequence without claiming map-rail accuracy
  • Screenshot captions on map frames — name the on-screen action: "在地图上发现附近好去处" not "强大功能"
  • Directions and route frames — describe the UI workflow shown (route preview, step list, ETA panel) without promising mainland turn-by-turn quality
  • Look Around marketing frames — caption the gallery hook (street-level preview, immersive map marketing) without overstating feature availability
  • Voice consistency — same 你/您 choice as your description across overlay lines and promotional text
