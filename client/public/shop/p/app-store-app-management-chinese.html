App Management-specific listing vocabulary — what translates differently

Manage-other-apps marketing on App Store pages uses vocabulary general MDM or Gatekeeper guides rarely cover. zh-Hans copy should reflect how Chinese App Store readers evaluate App Management permission claims:

应用管理 vs 应用管理权限 vs 更新其他 App

US pages say "App Management", "Manage other apps", or "Enable in System Settings → App Management." China storefront copy often uses 应用管理 for general App Management positioning aligned with Settings → Privacy & Security → App Management, 应用管理权限 when the hero frame shows the permission rationale or access scope, and 更新其他 App when English source names update-other-applications workflows. AppLocale picks commerce-idiomatic phrasing per frame — not 托管应用 on consumer TCC screenshots, not 门禁 on app-updater panels, and not 开发者工具 on manage-other-apps walkthroughs.

App Management vs Managed Apps MDM

Consumer App Management (应用管理) lets end users toggle per-app grants that let utilities update or manage other applications on a personal Mac. Managed Apps (托管应用) lets MDM administrators push, install, remove, and update apps on supervised or enrolled fleet devices. English shares "app management" phrasing — Chinese storefront copy must not conflate consumer 应用管理 TCC with 托管应用 MDM marketing. Managed Apps frames belong on the Managed Apps listing guide when MDM 应用分发 leads — not mixed into consumer App Management captions unless each frame is labeled honestly.

App Management vs Gatekeeper

App Management (应用管理) governs whether apps may update, install, or manage other applications. Gatekeeper (门禁) governs which software may run based on App Store, identified developer, or anywhere settings and notarization enforcement. An app updater needs App Management to act on other apps; Gatekeeper decides whether unsigned software may launch at all. Gatekeeper frames belong on the Gatekeeper listing guide when 门禁 leads — not mixed into App Management captions.

App Management vs Developer Tools

App Management (应用管理) lets app updaters, package managers, and installers manage other applications. Developer Tools (开发者工具) lets debugging utilities attach to other processes for development and profiling. Different TCC services with different buyer stories. Developer Tools frames belong on the Developer Tools listing guide when 开发者工具 leads.

App Management vs PPPC

Consumer App Management (应用管理) lets end users toggle per-app manage-other-apps grants in System Settings after consent. PPPC (隐私偏好策略控制) lets IT admins deploy PrivacyPreferencesPolicyControl payloads that pre-grant or deny App Management for named apps on enrolled Macs. PPPC frames and app-management-mdm grant intents belong on the PPPC listing guide when MDM TCC grants lead — not mixed into consumer App Management captions unless each frame is labeled honestly.
