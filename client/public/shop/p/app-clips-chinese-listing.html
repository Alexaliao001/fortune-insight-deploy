What China users see on an English App Clip card

A buyer in China who scans a link or taps a Smart App Banner may see a card that still shows Latin script while your product page reads native. Common failure modes:

  • English display title on a zh-Hans listing — the App Store product page says one thing; the clip card says another
  • Machine-translated subtitle — 56 characters of formal 翻译腔 that does not match your listing tone or category vocabulary
  • Generic English header reused from the full app name — "Quick Order" or "Try Now" instead of a context-specific clip title
  • Wrong call-to-action verb — Play selected for a utility clip, or View for an interactive game, confusing users before they tap
  • Untranslated text in the header image — Connect subtitle is Chinese but the 1800×1200 header still shows English headlines (a design fix, not a metadata paste)
  • Advanced experience left in English — default experience localized but a location-specific advanced card still shows English fallback

Better zh-Hans App Clip card copy reduces friction when users tap Open — it does not replace in-app UI localization, invocation URL testing, or Apple's choices about when to surface a clip.

Apple Connect field limits (App Clip card text)

Per Apple's App Clip launch experience documentation, localized text fields on the card are capped as follows. CJK characters count toward the same limits as Latin letters — plan shorter Chinese phrasing than English drafts.

  • Display title (header) — up to 30 characters; appears as the primary line on the App Clip card. Should name the clip context, not repeat your full app marketing tagline
  • Subtitle — up to 56 characters; secondary line explaining what happens when the user taps the action button
  • Call-to-action verb — Open, View, or Play (system-defined); Apple localizes the button label for the storefront language when the verb matches the experience type
  • Header image — 1800 × 1200 px PNG or JPEG; separate upload per localization, not a text field

Default App Clip experience covers digital invocations (links, banners). What good zh-Hans App Clip card copy looks like

The card appears for seconds before launch. Strong Chinese copy states context and outcome in plain language, aligned with your listing:

  • Display title (30 chars) — name the clip action: "扫码点单" not "欢迎使用我们的应用"
  • Subtitle (56 chars) — one crisp outcome: what the user gets without opening the full app first
  • Verb choice — Open for tools and ordering flows, Play for games and media, View for browse-only previews; pick once per experience in Connect
  • Voice match — same 你/您 habits and punctuation as your listing subtitle and description
  • No keyword stuffing — the subtitle slot is for user context, not ASO terms copied from your keywords field
