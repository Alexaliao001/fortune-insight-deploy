Computer Backup workflow vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Apple's device-backup workflow covers storefront language. Finder, Apple Devices, and App Store Connect actually separate local backup behavior from product-page copy:

  • Finder on Mac — connect iPhone or iPad via USB or Wi-Fi sync, select the device in the Finder sidebar, choose Back up your most important data on your Mac. Buyers run this on their Mac; it does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Apple Devices on Windows — successor to iTunes device management; backup iPhone or iPad to the PC with Back Up Now and encrypted backup options. Your listing may show this workflow on Windows screenshots; describing buyer-visible backup steps is listing copy, not engineering work.
  • Encrypted local backup — checkbox to encrypt backups with a password, required for restoring Health and Keychain data. Product-page claims about encrypted backup security are listing scope — not something AppLocale configures on buyer devices.
  • Back Up Now & last backup date — manual backup trigger and timestamp buyers see in Finder or Apple Devices. Caption fields explain backup freshness; managing buyer backup schedules is outside AppLocale.
  • Trust this computer — prompt when connecting a device to a new Mac or PC. Screenshot overlays that show the trust dialog are Computer Backup listing scope when local backup is the hero.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of Finder backup being enabled on their Mac.

A buyer who backs up to a Mac and a localized China listing are both important when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other.
