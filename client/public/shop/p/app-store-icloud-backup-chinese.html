iCloud Backup setting vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Apple's iCloud Backup workflow covers storefront language. Settings and App Store Connect actually separate device-backup behavior from product-page copy:

  • Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → iCloud Backup — the system toggle and status screen that backs up iPhone or iPad to iCloud. Buyers enable it on their devices; it does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Automatic iCloud Backup — nightly backup when the device is locked, charging, and connected to Wi‑Fi. Your listing may show this schedule on screenshots; describing buyer-visible backup timing is listing copy, not engineering work.
  • Back Up Now & last successful backup — manual backup trigger and timestamp buyers see on the iCloud Backup screen. Product-page claims about backup freshness or one-tap backup are listing scope — not something AppLocale configures on buyer devices.
  • iCloud storage for backups — storage meter and upgrade prompts when backup size exceeds available iCloud space. Description and caption fields explain value; managing buyer iCloud plans is outside AppLocale.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of iCloud Backup being enabled on their iPhone.

A buyer who uses iCloud Backup and a localized China listing are both important when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other.
