What Apple Scheduled Summary / Notification Summary is — and what your listing may claim

Apple ships Scheduled Summary (also marketed as Notification Summary in some en-US copy) under Settings → Notifications → Scheduled Summary. Non-time-sensitive notifications can bundle into a digest delivered at scheduled times — morning and evening by default — instead of arriving individually throughout the day. Time-sensitive notifications break through immediately and are not held for the digest. Western indie listings often show one or more of these surfaces on the US product page.   • Scheduled Summary master toggle (通知摘要 / 计划的摘要) — Settings → Notifications → Scheduled Summary enabled with delivery schedule visible. Gallery frames may show the toggle and time pickers.
  • Daily digest delivery (每日摘要) — marketing that bundled non-time-sensitive alerts arrive together at set times — not instant ping-by-ping delivery for every alert.
  • Deliver quietly until summary (安静送达直至摘要) — when your English gallery shows notifications held quietly until the next digest window.
  • Per-app summary participation (按 App 加入摘要) — Settings captures showing which apps send notifications into the scheduled digest versus immediate delivery.
  • Summary vs time-sensitive contrast — when your English listing honestly pairs digest scheduling with time-sensitive break-through alerts. Include time-sensitive vocabulary only when your US listing already shows that distinction.
