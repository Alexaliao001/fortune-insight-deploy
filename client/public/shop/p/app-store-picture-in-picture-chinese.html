PiP engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Picture in Picture capability covers storefront language. Xcode, entitlements, and Connect actually separate PiP behavior from product-page copy:

  • AVPictureInPictureController & AVKit — configured in your app to start and sustain floating video. Controls whether PiP appears on supported devices. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Background audio / video modes — Info.plist and entitlement flags for playback while backgrounded. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains PiP value to buyers.
  • In-app player chrome — play/pause labels, PiP button tooltips, and mini-player controls. Your listing may mention floating video; implementing PiP handlers is engineering work, not listing copy.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of PiP flags in the binary.

Working Picture in Picture in the binary and a localized China listing are both required when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other.

How is Picture in Picture listing copy different from Live Activities or SharePlay?

Picture in Picture is a floating video or audio window that stays on screen while the user switches apps — not Lock Screen Live Activities, not Dynamic Island live updates, not FaceTime SharePlay shared sessions. Screenshot captions should name 画中画, 小窗播放, or 切换应用继续观看 rather than ActivityKit timers or Watch Together overlays.

What character limits apply to zh-Hans Picture in Picture listing fields?

App name and subtitle are each up to 30 characters on the zh-Hans row. The keyword field allows up to 100 characters. Description and screenshot caption lines have longer limits but should stay concise — especially subtitle and overlay lines that must fit on-device mockups showing the floating video window.

Should I claim Picture in Picture on every device if my app only supports PiP on iPad?

No. Listing copy must match what your binary actually supports. If PiP works on iPad but not iPhone, zh-Hans description and screenshot captions should scope honestly — for example iPad 画中画 — not imply floating video on every device gallery frame.
