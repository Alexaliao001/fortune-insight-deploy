iCloud Drive setting vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Apple's iCloud Drive workflow covers storefront language. Settings, Files, and App Store Connect actually separate cloud-disk behavior from product-page copy:

  • Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → iCloud Drive — the system toggle that lets documents live in Apple's cloud disk. Buyers enable it on their devices; it does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Files → Browse → iCloud Drive — the cloud-disk location where folders and documents appear in the Files app. Your listing may show this path on screenshots; teaching buyers where files live is listing copy, not engineering work.
  • Desktop & Documents on Mac — when your Mac marketing mentions desktop and documents folders syncing through iCloud Drive. Product-page claims about Mac folder continuity are listing scope — not something AppLocale configures on buyer devices.
  • Offline-available files & folder sharing — buyer-facing benefits when documents stay in iCloud Drive and can be shared or opened offline. Description and caption fields explain value; implementing storage behavior is outside AppLocale.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of iCloud Drive being enabled on their iPhone.
