Parking Location vocabulary — what translates differently in zh-Hans

Parked-car location listings use terms general localization guides rarely cover. zh-Hans copy should reflect how Chinese App Store readers evaluate Parking Location features — not a literal paste of US marketing:

停车位置 vs 停车地点 vs 泊车位置

US pages use Parking Location, Parked Car Location, or Where I Parked. Chinese App Store copy commonly uses 停车位置 for parking location, 停车地点 for parking spot or place, and 泊车位置 for parked position — depending on whether you mean Apple's system parked-car pin, a manual drop pin, or a parking-garage level reminder. Pick the term buyers recognize on each screenshot frame.

我的车在哪 vs Find My 查找

Drivers searching for their parked car often type 我的车在哪 (where is my car). That is parked-car location intent — not Find My network item tracking (查找) for keys, bags, or AirTag accessories. If your app markets Where I Parked on Maps, zh-Hans copy should use parking-location vocabulary — not 查找 network badges unless your English listing already markets Find My item tracking accurately on separate frames.

Parked-car pin vs Vehicle Detection outlines

A parked-car pin on Apple Maps marks where you left the car — 停车标记 or 已停车辆 in system UI. Vehicle Detection in Magnifier draws live camera outlines around cars in the viewfinder — 车辆检测. A parking lot may appear in both screenshots, but the buyer story differs: saved parking spot on a map vs detecting vehicles with the camera.
