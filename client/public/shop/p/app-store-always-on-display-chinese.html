What good zh-Hans Always-On Display listing copy looks like

Listing fields should name the always-on surface your screenshots actually show — iPhone lock screen, Apple Watch face, or both — aligned with gallery order:

  • Subtitle — one concrete promise: “支持 iPhone 全天候显示” or “Apple Watch 表盘常亮可见” — not vague “提升效率”, and not iPhone AOD claims when only Watch ships
  • Keywords — mix category terms (时钟, 待办, 健身) with AOD intent (全天候显示, 常亮, 表盘) within Connect’s ~100-character zh-Hans budget; see Simplified Chinese keywords guide
  • Description — short paragraphs for dimmed lock screen glances and/or always-on watch face data — mirror screenshot sequence and match the hardware you actually support
  • Screenshot captions on AOD frames — name the on-screen action: “息屏也能一眼看到待办” or “抬腕放下，数据仍常亮” — not “Always-On Display” in Latin script
  • Sample widget or complication titles in caption lines — when your gallery shows example always-on data, caption sheet lists zh-Hans equivalents for designer re-export
  • Voice consistency — same 你/您 choice as your description across overlay lines and promotional text
