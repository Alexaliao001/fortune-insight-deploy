Contact Poster engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Apple's Contact Poster rollout covers storefront language. iOS, system frameworks, and Connect actually separate poster design behavior from product-page copy:

  • Contact Posters (iOS 17+) — system feature in Contacts and Photos that lets users design a full-screen poster with photo, font, and color for a contact. The poster appears on incoming calls and on the contact detail card. Controls in-device poster appearance — not App Store language.
  • Contacts poster editor — Customize Contact Poster sheet with photo picker, font style, color gradient, and layout options. Your listing may show the editor; building poster UI is engineering work, not Connect metadata.
