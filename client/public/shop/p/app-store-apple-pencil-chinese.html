What good zh-Hans Apple Pencil listing copy looks like

Pencil-centric listings name the stylus workflow in plain zh-Hans — not a word-for-word mirror of English brochure lines:

  • Subtitle — one clause on handwriting, sketching, or annotation outcome; room for Pencil-adjacent search terms within 30 characters

  • Voice match — same 你/您 and punctuation habits as your other zh-Hans fields
