iCloud Photos setting vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Apple's iCloud Photos workflow covers storefront language. Settings and App Store Connect actually separate photo-library sync behavior from product-page copy:

  • Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → iCloud Photos — the system toggle that uploads and syncs the personal photo library to iCloud. Buyers enable it on their devices; it does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Automatic photo and video upload — the on-device library uploads to iCloud when connected. Your listing may show this sync story on screenshots; describing buyer-visible library sync is listing copy, not engineering work.
  • Sync across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and iCloud.com — the same iCloud Photos library available on every signed-in device and at icloud.com/photos. Product-page claims about cross-device photo access are listing scope — not something AppLocale configures on buyer devices.
