Podcasts-specific listing copy — what translates differently

Podcasts-app-centric listings use vocabulary general audio guides rarely cover. zh-Hans copy should reflect how Chinese App Store readers describe podcast workflows — not a literal paste of US marketing:

播客 vs 播客应用

US pages say “Apple Podcasts” or “Podcasts app.” China storefront copy often uses 播客 for the podcast category and listener intent, and 播客应用 when naming the app category or Podcasts home integration on gallery frames. AppLocale aligns phrasing with each screenshot — not one blanket term for every overlay.

节目 vs 单集

Show detail pages, show artwork, and season headers need 节目 — the show a listener follows. Episode lists, Now Playing screens, chapter markers, and Share Episode cards need 单集 — individual episodes. Do not use 音乐 or 歌曲 on podcast episode frames — those belong to Apple Music marketing.

订阅 vs 订阅节目 vs 跟踪播客

Paid channel subscriptions and show-level subscribe buttons on Podcasts show pages use 订阅 or 订阅节目 — not English “Subscribe” left on overlays. Free follow-show actions, library entries for shows you follow, and “added to library” flows use 跟踪播客. AppLocale matches the button or badge visible on each frame — subscribe and follow are different buyer actions on the Podcasts app.

Library, stations & Search in Podcasts

Podcast library and shows-you-follow shelves need library vocabulary Chinese listeners expect — 播客库, 已关注的节目 — rather than English “Library” or “Shows You Follow.” Custom stations and curated queues need station phrasing aligned to the screenshot. Search-in-Podcasts frames need concise captions — 在播客中搜索 — not generic “Find anything” slogans.

Transcripts, chapters, playback speed & sleep timer

Transcript panels and chapter lists on podcast Now Playing screens need 文稿 / 章节 vocabulary — distinct from Live Voicemail call transcription. Playback speed and sleep timer controls on a podcast episode need tool labels buyers recognize — 播放速度, 睡眠定时 — not English micro-copy burned into captions.

Share Episode / Share Show cards

Share sheets and preview cards for episodes or shows need concise zh-Hans captions — 分享单集, 分享节目 — rather than English “Share Episode” or “Share Show” that breaks gallery consistency.

Subtitle and keyword packing

The 30-character subtitle and 100-character keyword field need Podcasts-relevant search terms Chinese buyers type — see Simplified Chinese keywords & subtitle for field mechanics. Terms like 播客, 播客应用, 节目, 单集, 订阅, and 跟踪播客 each signal different intent from Music terms like 音乐 or Fitness terms like 健身.
