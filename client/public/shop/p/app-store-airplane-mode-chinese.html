FAQ

What is Airplane Mode and where do buyers find it?

Airplane Mode is an iOS radio kill-switch — disable cellular, Wi‑Fi, and Bluetooth together. Buyers toggle it from Control Center (airplane icon) or Settings → Airplane Mode. Simplified Chinese labels it 飞行模式.

Does Airplane Mode awareness in my app auto-translate my China listing?

No. Offline detection and radio toggles control on-device behavior — they do not create or fill a Simplified Chinese localization row. Empty or English zh-Hans fields mean China buyers see en-US fallback.

How is Airplane Mode different from Control Center?

Control Center is the quick-settings panel — Add Control, Lock Screen controls, customizable toggles — 控制中心. Airplane Mode is one toggle on that panel that kills all radios — 飞行模式. See our Control Center listing guide for panel customization scope.

How is Airplane Mode different from Focus or Do Not Disturb?

Focus and Do Not Disturb silence notifications — 勿扰, 专注模式. Airplane Mode disables cellular, Wi‑Fi, and Bluetooth radios — 飞行模式. See our Focus listing guide for notification silencing scope.

How is Airplane Mode different from Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, or Cellular toggles alone?

Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, and Cellular each control one radio — 无线局域网, 蓝牙, 蜂窝网络. Airplane Mode turns all three off together. Buyers may re-enable Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth individually while Airplane Mode stays on.

How is Airplane Mode different from Low Power Mode?

Low Power Mode saves battery — 低电量模式 — under Settings → Battery. Airplane Mode kills radios for travel and offline use — 飞行模式. See our Low Power Mode listing guide for battery scope.
