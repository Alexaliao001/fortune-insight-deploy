App Privacy Report on the device vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume privacy transparency features cover storefront language. iOS Settings and App Store Connect actually separate activity reporting from product-page copy:

  • App Privacy Report (App隐私报告 / 隐私报告) — on-device dashboard under Settings → Privacy & Security → App Privacy Report. Summarizes App Activity — which apps accessed contacts, sensors, microphone, or camera — and Network Activity — domains contacted and contact frequency. System Settings behavior — does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Network Activity charts — domain contact frequency lists buyers use to see which apps contacted which servers. Listing copy may reference 网络活动 and 域名联系 when your gallery shows those charts.
  • Sensor and permission access timelines — microphone access (麦克风访问), camera access (摄像头访问), contacts access (通讯录访问), and broader sensor access (传感器访问) rows inside App Activity. Match frame-by-frame to what your screenshot shows.
  • MetricKit and privacy manifests — developer-side diagnostics and required-reason API declarations in the binary. Engineering inside Xcode — not listing rewrite.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of App Privacy Report marketing on your US gallery.

An app that references App Privacy Report in onboarding and a localized China listing are both valuable — neither replaces the other. FAQ

What is Apple App Privacy Report and why does it matter for listing copy?

App Privacy Report is an on-device dashboard under Settings → Privacy & Security → App Privacy Report. It shows App Activity — contacts, sensors, microphone, and camera access — and Network Activity — domains apps contacted and contact frequency. Marketing those surfaces on your en-US page does not auto-fill a zh-Hans localization row.

Is App Privacy Report the same as Privacy Nutrition Labels?

No. Privacy Nutrition Labels are App Store Connect questionnaire disclosures for the Privacy Details panel on the product page. App Privacy Report is the on-device Settings activity log buyers open after install. See our privacy nutrition labels listing guide for Connect filing scope — not App Privacy Report UI marketing.

Is App Privacy Report the same as App Tracking Transparency?

No. ATT is the Ask App Not to Track / Allow Tracking permission sheet for cross-app tracking. App Privacy Report shows retrospective network activity and sensor-access timelines — not the ATT prompt. See our ATT listing guide when tracking permission is your lead claim.

Is App Privacy Report the same as Content & Privacy Restrictions?

No. Content & Privacy Restrictions is a Screen Time parental gate for content ratings and privacy permission toggles parents configure for children. App Privacy Report is a device-wide activity report any user can open. See our Content & Privacy Restrictions listing guide for parental restriction scope.

Does AppLocale implement MetricKit or App Privacy Report APIs?

No. We deliver Connect listing metadata copy only — name, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text when included, and screenshot caption lines. MetricKit diagnostics, privacy manifests, and App Privacy Report API integration are engineering tasks outside AppLocale scope.

What zh-Hans terms should App Privacy Report listings use?
