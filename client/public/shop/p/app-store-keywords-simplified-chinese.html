Title, subtitle, and the 100-character keyword field

App Store Connect treats three zh-Hans inputs as separate jobs. The title (app name, commonly 30 characters) is what buyers see first in Search — lead with category and outcome, not pinyin-only branding or adjective stacks. The subtitle adds one fact the title does not carry: audience, workflow, or pricing — without mirroring the same nouns. The keyword field is hidden and capped at 100 characters: comma-separated terms, no spaces after commas, no words already visible in title or subtitle.

  • Put synonyms, problem phrases, and English loanwords users actually search in zh-Hans into the keyword field
  • Cut duplicate nouns — repeating “效率” in title, subtitle, and keywords wastes characters Apple may ignore
  • Skip competitor names, trending words your screens do not deliver, and superlatives you cannot substantiate
  • Align meaning with your description opening so the promise matches what screenshots show
