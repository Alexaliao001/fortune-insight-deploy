What good zh-Hans StandBy listing copy looks like

  • Subtitle — one concrete promise: “横屏充电待机显示” or “夜间充电，信息一目了然” — not vague “提升效率”
  • Keywords — mix category terms (时钟, 待办, 照片) with StandBy intent (待机显示, 横屏) within Connect’s ~100-character zh-Hans budget; see Simplified Chinese keywords guide
  • Description — short paragraphs for landscape charging, carousel pages, and what appears on the nightstand — mirror screenshot sequence
  • Screenshot captions on StandBy frames — name the on-screen action: “横屏充电时显示待办与天气” not “Works with StandBy”
  • Sample dashboard titles in caption lines — when your gallery shows example nightstand data, caption sheet lists zh-Hans equivalents for designer re-export
  • Voice consistency — same 你/您 choice as your description across overlay lines and promotional text
