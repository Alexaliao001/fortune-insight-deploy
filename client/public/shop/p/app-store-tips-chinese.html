Tips-specific vocabulary — what translates differently in zh-Hans

Tips-style listings use terms general localization guides rarely cover. zh-Hans copy should reflect how Chinese App Store readers evaluate how-to and discovery features — not a literal paste of US marketing:

提示 (Tips) vs 使用技巧 (how-to techniques)

Apple’s Chinese system UI uses 提示 for the Tips app name and branded tip surfaces. 使用技巧 fits step-by-step how-to guides and feature walkthrough cards when the frame shows numbered instructions. Pick the term buyers recognize on each screenshot — a Tips home row may use 提示; a multi-step tutorial card may use 使用技巧 when accurate.

窍门 (quick tricks) and 精选合集 (featured collections)

US pages use tips and tricks for casual quick-trick cards and Featured Collection for curated tip bundles. Chinese App Store copy commonly uses 窍门 or 小技巧 for bite-size trick cards and 精选合集 for collection shelves when your gallery actually shows those rows. Do not promise system Tips integration in listing copy if your product only ships custom tutorial content without Apple Tips branding.

Try it and walkthrough chrome

Interactive Try it badges and hands-on tutorial entry points need concise zh-Hans overlays — often 立即体验, 试试看, or 跟着做 depending on frame context. Step-by-step walkthrough headlines should mirror screenshot order with natural Chinese imperatives, not word-for-word “How to…” calques.
