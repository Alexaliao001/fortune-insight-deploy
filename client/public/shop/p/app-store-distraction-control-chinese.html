FAQ

What is Safari Distraction Control and why does it matter for App Store listing copy?

Safari Distraction Control is an iOS 18+ Safari feature that lets users hide distracting items on the live webpage — sign-in banners, cookie popups, newsletter overlays, sidebars — by choosing Hide Distracting Items from the Page Menu and tapping elements to remove. English marketing on your en-US page does not auto-fill the zh-Hans row in App Store Connect.

How is Safari Distraction Control listing copy different from Reader Mode?

Reader Mode restyles the article into a clean reading layout — 阅读器模式. Safari Distraction Control hides specific items the user taps on the live webpage — 干扰控制 / 隐藏干扰项.
