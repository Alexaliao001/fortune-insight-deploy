Phone app vocabulary — 电话, 拨号键盘, 最近通话, 收藏, 通讯录, and 语音信箱

Phone-centric listings use vocabulary general utility guides rarely cover:

电话 vs 电话应用 vs literal "Phone"

US pages use "Phone", "Dialer", or "Calling app." China storefront copy often uses 电话 or 电话应用 for the dialer home surface — without implying you are Apple's built-in Phone.app unless your English listing honestly claims full system dialer replacement.

拨号键盘, 最近通话, and 收藏

Gallery frames showing the numeric pad should use 拨号键盘 — not generic 打电话 when the frame is clearly the keypad tab. Recents call logs map to 最近通话. Speed-dial or starred contacts on the Favorites tab map to 收藏 — distinct from 通讯录 when you mean the Contacts tab inside Phone.

通讯录 on Phone frames vs standalone Contacts app

When your screenshot shows the Contacts tab inside Phone — browse and call saved people — 通讯录 or 通讯录标签 is appropriate. When your screenshot shows Add to Contacts or Save contact sheets, that is address-book saving marketing — see Add to Contacts listing guide, not this Phone dialer page.

语音信箱 vs Live Voicemail transcription

The voicemail tab listing missed calls and playing back messages uses 语音信箱. Real-time transcription while a caller is still speaking into voicemail is Live Voicemail marketing — see Live Voicemail listing guide when transcription is the lead frame, not a simple voicemail inbox list.

双卡 and 来电 on dual-SIM call screens

Dual SIM line badges and line pickers on Phone-style call screens use 双卡 or 双卡双待 when accurate. Incoming-call mockups use 来电 — distinct from 来电筛选 Call Screening terminology reserved for reason-before-ringing flows.

Subtitle and keyword packing

The 30-character subtitle and 100-character keyword field need Phone search terms Chinese buyers type — see Simplified Chinese keywords & subtitle for field mechanics. Terms like 电话, 拨号键盘, and 最近通话 each signal different intent from 来电筛选 screening terms or 通话录音 recording keywords.
