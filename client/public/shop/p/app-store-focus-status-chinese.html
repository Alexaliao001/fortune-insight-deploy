What Apple Focus Status is — and what contacts actually see

Apple ships Focus Status under Settings → Focus → Focus Status (labeled Share Focus Status in some iOS versions) and per-mode under Settings → Focus → [a Focus] → Focus Status. When enabled, approved contacts in Messages can see that you have notifications silenced — they see Focus or a localized equivalent instead of a last-seen time or delivery receipt. They do not see which Focus mode is active. Western indie listings often show one or more of these surfaces on the US product page.
