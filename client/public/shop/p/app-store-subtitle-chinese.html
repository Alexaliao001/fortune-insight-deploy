Subtitle vs keyword field — visible vs hidden

App Store Connect gives zh-Hans listings two different search inputs. The subtitle sits under your app name on the product page and in Search results — buyers read it while scrolling. Apple’s limit is commonly 30 characters for zh-Hans — verify in Connect before you save. The keyword field is hidden, comma-separated, and capped at 100 characters — a separate job with different rules.

US subtitle habits that fail in zh-Hans

Indie US teams often treat the en-US subtitle as a second keyword line. Common patterns that do not land when pasted into zh-Hans:

  • Feature stacks — “Track · Plan · Focus” or comma-separated adjectives (“Smart · Simple · Fast”) that read as brochure filler in Chinese
  • Superlatives without proof — “The best”, “#1”, “ultimate” equivalents (“最好”, “终极”) that Chinese skim readers ignore
  • Keyword mirroring — repeating nouns already in the title because US ASO guides say “put keywords in subtitle”
  • Literal en-US translation — slogan lines that sound native in English but formal or hollow in Simplified Chinese
  • Pinyin-only branding — romanization where Chinese users expect category or outcome words they actually search

On the China storefront, the subtitle earns a second glance when it adds one fact the title does not carry — not when it stacks more keywords.

What a good zh-Hans subtitle carries

Within 30 characters, strong Chinese subtitles usually name one of these — without repeating the title:

  • Audience — who the app is for (“独立开发者”, “考研党”, “小团队”)
  • Workflow — one concrete use case (“离线 PDF 批注”, “番茄钟专注”)
  • Pricing model — when it matters to the buyer (“免费 · 内购解锁”, “一次买断”)
  • Platform or format — when the title is brand-heavy (“iPhone 小组件”, “Mac 菜单栏”)

Write for a Chinese skim reader deciding whether to tap your result — not for a keyword-density checklist copied from en-US ASO blogs.

Common zh-Hans subtitle mistakes

  • Leaving the subtitle empty while the title alone does not explain the category
  • Copying the en-US subtitle word-for-word through machine translation
  • Stuffing search terms that belong in the hidden keyword field — visible duplication wastes characters Apple may de-weight
  • Using middle-dot separators (“·”) as decoration without adding information
  • Formal false cognates (“专业级”, “高效能”) that read as template copy from translation tools
  • Exceeding the character limit and getting truncated mid-phrase in Search results

A clearer subtitle reduces bounce on the product page — it does not replace keyword field work, description copy, or in-app UI localization.
