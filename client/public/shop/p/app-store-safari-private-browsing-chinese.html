Safari Private Browsing behavior vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Safari integration covers storefront language. iOS, WebKit, and Connect actually separate Private Browsing behavior from product-page copy:

  • Private Browsing window & dark chrome — system Safari UI where buyers open a private tab, see the dark address bar, and browse without saving history to the device. Controls what Safari users see while browsing. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Lock Private Browsing — Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode required to unlock open private tabs when the device locks. Listing copy names that lock-tab protection; building the lock sheet is system Safari behavior, not Connect metadata.
  • No history & no leftover tabs — private tabs do not appear in History, and closing the last private tab ends the session. zh-Hans overlays should name 无痕浏览 and no-history vocabulary when your English frames show that promise.
  • Separate from everyday tabs — private tabs live in a distinct tab group from regular browsing. Product-page copy should match what your gallery shows — a private-window demo is not a Safari Profile picker unless that is what the screenshot depicts.
  • WebKit & SFSafariViewController — in-app code that opens web content. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains Private Browsing-aware workflows to buyers.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of Safari integration in the app.
