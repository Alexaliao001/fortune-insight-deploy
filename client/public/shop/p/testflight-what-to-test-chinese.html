What to Test vs What's New vs the public listing

Three surfaces indie teams conflate when China enters the picture:

  • TestFlight What to Test — beta-only instructions shown inside the TestFlight app before install. Updated per build in App Store Connect under TestFlight → Build → Test Information. China testers read this on their phone in zh-Hans locale; English-only notes feel like an unfinished product.
  • App Store What's New — public release notes on the product page after App Store release. A different field, different audience, different lifecycle. See our What's New in Simplified Chinese guide if you need public update notes only — this page is about beta instructions, not that field.
  • Public listing metadata — name, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshot captions on the zh-Hans row buyers will see at launch. TestFlight does not replace this work; a polished beta with an English storefront still converts poorly on day one.

Why China testers notice English beta notes

Mainland testers often join through a link you posted in a WeChat group, a product forum, or a diaspora channel — not through your US mailing list. They install TestFlight, accept the invite, and land on a build card written like an internal sprint note:

  • Jargon from your en-US roadmap (“refactor sync layer”, “fix edge case in onboarding v2”) with no Chinese equivalent
  • Assumed context from prior builds testers in China never saw
  • Bug-report instructions in English only — feedback quality drops when testers guess what you want
  • Feature names that match your English UI because in-app strings are not localized yet
  • No mention of China-specific flows (SMS login, WeChat share sheet, regional payment copy) you actually need tested

Clear zh-Hans What to Test notes do not localize your app binary. They do tell China testers exactly which screens to open and what to screenshot back. That is copy work — not TestFlight configuration.

What good zh-Hans What to Test notes look like

Beta instructions should read like a short QA script, not marketing brochure copy:

  • Lead with the one or two flows you need eyes on this build — “请测试新建项目 → 邀请协作者 → 离线同步恢复”
  • Call out known broken areas so testers do not file duplicate crashes you already track
  • Specify feedback channel in plain Chinese if you want structured reports (email, form link, screenshot expectations)
  • Match vocabulary to the zh-Hans listing you will ship — inconsistent product names between beta notes and storefront confuse early adopters
  • Keep each build’s notes short; testers skim on mobile before tapping Install

When the public listing is still English on the China storefront, testers who look you up on the App Store see the same neglect. Pair beta-note rewrites with a complete zh-Hans row — see China App Store still shows English for the fallback problem.
