PassKit engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Apple Pay capability covers storefront language. Xcode, Developer portal, and Connect actually separate checkout behavior from product-page copy:

  • PassKit & PKPayment — configured in your app to present Apple Pay sheets and authorize payments. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Core NFC & NFCNDEF — reader/writer sessions for tap-to-pay or tag workflows in the binary. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains checkout value to buyers.
  • Tap to Pay on iPhone (merchant) — terminal-style acceptance for eligible merchants under Settings → Wallet & Apple Pay → Tap to Pay on iPhone. Separate buyer story from consumer in-app Apple Pay checkout — see Tap to Pay on iPhone merchant listing guide.
  • Express Transit & Express Mode (commuter) — ride public transit without authentication under Settings → Wallet & Apple Pay → Express Transit Mode. Separate gate-tap story from retail checkout — see Express Transit listing guide.
  • Apple Pay Later (BNPL checkout) — buy now pay later at Apple Pay checkout, pay in 4 over six weeks, apply in Wallet. Separate installment story from standard tap-to-pay checkout — see Apple Pay Later listing guide.
  • Payment processor & merchant ID setup — Stripe, Adyen, Braintree, or other PSP onboarding. AppLocale does not configure payment rails.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of Apple Pay flags in the binary.

Working Apple Pay or NFC checkout in the binary and a localized China listing are both required when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other.

  • Assuming Apple Pay integration localizes automatically — PassKit wired in Xcode; zh-Hans row never added or left blank
  • Only translating the description — subtitle, keywords, and Apple Pay screenshot caption lines still English
  • Promising Apple Pay in mainland China — listing claims universal Apple Pay availability when your English source only markets the checkout UI surface
  • Conflating listing copy with in-app checkout strings — Pay sheet labels and button titles inside the binary are app localization, not Connect metadata
  • English "Apple Pay" on zh-Hans screenshots — Connect caption fields are Chinese but burned-in payment mockups on PNG exports are still Latin
  • Mixing IAP tier names with Apple Pay marketing — purchase sheet product names belong in IAP localization rows, not Apple Pay button caption lines
  • What's New still English after Apple Pay launch — see What's New in Simplified Chinese when checkout shipped in a recent version
  • Expecting AppLocale to configure PassKit or NFC entitlements — merchant ID, PKPayment networks, and Core NFC session code are outside listing rewrite
