阅读器 vs Reader mode

Screenshot galleries often label distraction-free reading: Safari Reader layouts, clean article views, or "Reader Mode" badges. zh-Hans overlays typically use 阅读器 or 阅读模式 — not a literal "Reader" transliteration. Match the reading workflow your English listing and screenshots actually show.
