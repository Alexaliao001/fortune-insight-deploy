How to turn on Apple Watch Mirroring — and why listing copy is a separate job

Teams assume accessibility features cover storefront language. iOS Settings, Control Center, and App Store Connect actually separate Watch Mirroring behavior from product-page copy:

  • Settings → Accessibility → Apple Watch Mirroring — system toggle that starts mirroring the paired Apple Watch display onto iPhone. Users see the live watch face, apps, and controls on the iPhone screen and can tap to interact. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Control Center shortcut — add Apple Watch Mirroring under Settings → Control Center for one-tap start and stop. Gallery frames may show the Control Center tile — listing copy should name 控制中心 / Apple Watch 镜像 when that is what the screenshot depicts.
  • Low vision & motor accessibility — Apple positions Watch Mirroring for users who need a larger view of the watch UI or find iPhone touch targets easier than the Watch screen. Marketing may reference 低视力, 辅助功能, or 在 iPhone 上操控 Apple Watch when your English page already claims those outcomes.

  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of Watch Mirroring support in your app marketing.

FAQ

What is Apple Watch Mirroring and why does it matter for App Store listing copy?

Apple Watch Mirroring mirrors the paired Apple Watch display onto iPhone so users can see and control the watch on a larger screen — primarily an accessibility feature for low vision and motor difficulties. Turn it on under Settings → Accessibility → Apple Watch Mirroring, or add it to Control Center. English marketing on your en-US page does not auto-fill the zh-Hans row in App Store Connect.

How is Apple Watch Mirroring listing copy different from iPhone Mirroring on Mac?

iPhone Mirroring shows and controls a nearby iPhone from a Mac window — iPhone 镜像. Apple Watch Mirroring shows and controls the Watch from iPhone — 手表镜像 / Apple Watch 镜像.
