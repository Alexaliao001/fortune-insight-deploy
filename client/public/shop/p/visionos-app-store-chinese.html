What good zh-Hans visionOS listing copy looks like

visionOS product page copy should name what the buyer experiences in spatial context — not a copy-paste of iPhone slogans:

  • Name and subtitle — when platform-specific fields are set, use concise Chinese that matches how Vision Pro users search; avoid English codenames unless trademark requires them
  • Promotional text — one timely hook (launch, season, feature) within the 170-character cap; updatable without a new binary when you maintain a visionOS-specific promo line

zh-Hans spatial captions can differ idiomatically from en-US lines when the on-screen feature is the same — avoid mirroring comma-heavy English brochure phrasing onto immersive art.
