ATT engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume ATT capability covers storefront language. Xcode, Developer portal, and Connect actually separate tracking behavior from product-page copy:

  • ATTrackingManager & IDFA access — in-app code that calls requestTrackingAuthorization and reads the advertising identifier when authorized. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • NSUserTrackingUsageDescription — Info.plist usage string shown before the system ATT sheet. In-app and binary localization, not Connect metadata.
  • App Privacy questionnaire & nutrition labels — structured disclosures Apple renders in Privacy Details. Separate from ATT prompt marketing on screenshots. See privacy nutrition labels listing guide — not this page.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of ATT implementation in the binary.

Working ATT in the binary and a localized China listing are both required when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other.

ATT-specific listing copy — what translates differently

Tracking-permission listings use vocabulary general utility guides rarely cover. zh-Hans copy should reflect how Chinese App Store readers describe ATT opt-in — not a literal paste of US marketing:

Ask App Not to Track and Allow Tracking phrasing

US pages use "Ask App Not to Track", "Allow Tracking", or "App Tracking Transparency" on onboarding mockups. Chinese buyers expect concise 应用跟踪透明度, 允许应用跟踪, or 跟踪权限说明 phrasing rather than English slogans left on screenshot overlays.

Tracking context without selling tracking as a feature

Description and caption lines should explain why the app may request tracking permission — relevant ads, attribution, personalized offers — without implying buyers benefit from opting in, without promising opt-in rates, and without marketing cross-app tracking as a paid product feature. Common mistakes on ATT-themed zh-Hans listings

  • Assuming ATT implementation localizes automatically — ATTrackingManager wired in Xcode; zh-Hans row never added or left blank
  • Only translating the description — subtitle, keywords, and Ask App Not to Track screenshot caption lines still English
  • Conflating ATT listing copy with nutrition labels — Privacy Details disclosures and ATT prompt marketing are different surfaces with different shop guides
  • Conflating ATT with push permission copy — Allow Notifications screenshots need the push permission guide, not ATT phrasing
  • Selling tracking as a feature — listing claims "better ads with tracking enabled" when your app should describe permission context neutrally
  • Conflating listing copy with NSUserTrackingUsageDescription — pre-prompt modal strings are binary localization, not Connect metadata
  • English Allow Tracking on zh-Hans screenshots — Connect caption fields are Chinese but burned-in permission mockups on PNG exports are still Latin
  • Expecting AppLocale to implement ATTrackingManager — prompt engineering, IDFA access, and SKAdNetwork configuration are outside listing rewrite
