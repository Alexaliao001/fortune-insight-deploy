Unlisted vs Custom App vs public listing

Three distribution models, one localization matrix:

  • Public App Store listing — discoverable via Search and browse. Organic traffic depends on zh-Hans ASO fields.
  • Unlisted App Store app — available only via direct link you share. Not in Search results, but the link opens a full product page with name, subtitle, description, and screenshots.
  • Custom App (ABM / ASM) — assigned to specific organization Apple IDs. IT admins and managed users see the same product-page fields when reviewing or installing the app.

In all three cases, App Store Connect stores metadata per localization row — en-US, zh-Hans, etc. Distribution method does not auto-translate your copy. An unlisted link shared with a Shanghai office still serves English fallback when zh-Hans fields are empty.

Why English product pages hurt B2B rollouts in China

Enterprise and partner installs skip consumer Search, but humans still read the page:

  • IT review — admins compare your English description against internal security questionnaires written in Chinese
  • Procurement — buyers forward the App Store link to stakeholders who expect Simplified Chinese vendor copy
  • Employee trust — a field tool assigned through MDM looks unofficial when the install screen is English-only
  • Mixed galleries — Chinese screenshot art with English caption strings signals an unfinished localization pass
  • Keyword waste — for unlisted apps that still expose a keyword field, empty zh-Hans means you cannot reserve Chinese terms even if Search volume is low

Better zh-Hans product-page copy reduces friction during review — it does not replace ABM org assignment, MDM packaging, or in-app UI translation.

A Custom App is distributed to specific organizations through Apple Business Manager or Apple School Manager — often B2B or internal tools. A Custom Product Page (CPP) is a marketing variant of a public app's default listing for paid ads or Product Page Optimization.
