Maximum Capacity vs Peak Performance Capability

Maximum Capacity shows the percentage relative to when the battery was new; Peak Performance Capability indicates whether the device can deliver full performance or has experienced unexpected shutdowns. zh-Hans listing copy should use 最大容量 and 峰值性能能力 accurately — not interchange them or imply your app can restore capacity percentages Apple reports in Settings.
