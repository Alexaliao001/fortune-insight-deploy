What ASA opens — and what it does not translate

Apple Search Ads is an auction on App Store search results. When someone taps your ad on the China storefront, Apple sends them to:

  • Your default product page — the standard listing every organic visitor sees, pulled from the zh-Hans localization row when that row is complete
  • Or a Custom Product Page URL — if you assigned one in the ad group; that CPP has its own subtitle, description, promotional text, and screenshot set per locale

ASA does not rewrite your Connect metadata. Ad creative text in the Ads dashboard is separate from the product page fields Review approved. A zh-Hans ad headline can promise “离线笔记” while the landing subtitle still reads “Offline notes for busy teams” — you pay for the mismatch.

Empty or English-only zh-Hans rows fall back to another locale — usually en-US. That is why campaigns “work in the US” but feel broken in China even when keywords match.

Default listing vs Custom Product Page for China ASA

Most indie teams start ASA on the default listing. Fix these zh-Hans fields before you scale spend:

  • Name and subtitle — what appears under the app icon on the landing page (~30 characters each for zh-Hans)
  • Keywords field — hidden 100-character pack aligned with ASA exact and broad match terms (see Simplified Chinese keywords)
  • Description and promotional text — long body and 170-character header slot buyers scroll past after the tap
  • Screenshot sets per device size — the gallery ASA shows; not your en-US uploads

When you attach a Custom Product Page to an ad group — common for segment tests or seasonal offers — the default zh-Hans row no longer controls that click. Each CPP needs its own zh-Hans localization matrix. Localizing the default listing does not localize CPPs. See Custom Product Page in Simplified Chinese for CPP-specific fields.

Practical order: fill default zh-Hans for brand and category search campaigns; add one localized CPP when a single ad angle (student offer, feature vertical, competitor conquest) needs different subtitle and screenshots than the default page.

Ad creative vs Connect localization row

Apple Search Ads lets you write ad variations — default ad, custom text, Creative Sets tied to screenshot assets. Three layers often drift apart:

  • ASA ad text — short lines shown in search results above your app name; editable in searchads.apple.com
  • Product page metadata — subtitle, description, promo text stored in App Store Connect under zh-Hans
  • Creative Set screenshots — assets uploaded in ASA that may differ from Connect screenshot galleries

Review approves Connect fields, not your ASA dashboard copy. You can run zh-Hans ad text while Connect still shows English — Apple will serve the ad, but post-tap trust collapses when languages disagree.

Align in this order: (1) zh-Hans Connect row for whichever URL the ad opens, (2) ASA ad text that echoes the same promise as subtitle and first screenshot caption, (3) Creative Set frames that match the zh-Hans gallery or CPP screenshots you uploaded in Connect. We help with step (1) — Connect paste-ready copy — not ASA dashboard operations.

Simplified Chinese keywords vs pinyin in ASA

Mainland users search the App Store in Simplified Chinese characters — 番茄钟, 冥想, 记账 — not romanized pinyin strings like “fanqiezhong” or English category words alone.

  • ASA keyword list — bid on native zh-Hans terms your audience types; use Search Match cautiously until the landing page is zh-Hans
  • Connect keyword field — separate 100-character slot for organic indexing; should reinforce ASA themes without duplicate stuffing across title, subtitle, and keywords (field-splitting rules differ from paid — organic guide: ASO title, subtitle, keywords)
  • Negative keywords — exclude English-only queries once zh-Hans landing is live; we do not configure these in your ASA account

Pinyin bids occasionally match autocorrect paths, but they are not a substitute for a readable zh-Hans listing. If your ASA report shows taps on Chinese queries and installs stall, check the product page language before you raise bids.

Screenshot sets ASA uses on the China storefront

After the tap, buyers see screenshots from the localization tied to the landing URL:

  • Default listing — zh-Hans screenshot slots per device class (6.7", 6.5", iPad, etc.) under App Information → Screenshots for Chinese (Simplified)
  • Custom Product Page — screenshots assigned to that CPP’s zh-Hans row, independent from default galleries
  • Creative Sets in ASA — can override which frames appear in the ad unit itself; the full product page gallery still comes from Connect

Common ASA waste pattern: Chinese overlay on screenshot one from a design tool, but Connect still stores English caption strings — or zh-Hans slots are empty so Apple serves en-US captures. Pre-flight checklist before you scale China ASA spend

  1. Confirm landing URL — default product page or specific CPP per ad group in searchads.apple.com
  2. Audit zh-Hans row for that URL — name, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text saved and within character limits

FAQ

Why does my Apple Search Ads China campaign show English on the product page?

Apple Search Ads sends the tap to your default App Store product page or a linked Custom Product Page. If the zh-Hans localization row in App Store Connect is empty, incomplete, or still English, buyers see en-US fallback even when the ad keyword and creative are in Simplified Chinese. ASA does not auto-translate your listing — Connect does.

Should I localize the default listing or a Custom Product Page for China ASA?

If your ad group uses the default product page URL, fix the zh-Hans default listing row first — name, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, and screenshot sets. If you deep-link to a Custom Product Page in ASA, that CPP needs its own zh-Hans row; default listing localization does not flow into CPPs. Many teams localize default listing for broad search terms and one CPP for a specific campaign angle.

Do Apple Search Ads keywords need to be in Simplified Chinese or pinyin?

For China storefront campaigns, bid on terms users actually type in Search — usually Simplified Chinese characters for category and feature queries, not romanized pinyin alone. Your App Store Connect keyword field for zh-Hans should pack native Chinese terms aligned with your ASA keyword list; pinyin rarely matches how mainland users search.

Which screenshot set does Apple Search Ads use on the China storefront?

ASA opens the screenshot gallery assigned to the default zh-Hans localization or to the Custom Product Page you linked in the ad group — not your en-US gallery. If zh-Hans screenshot slots are empty, Apple falls back to another locale, often English UI captures with mismatched overlay captions.
