Content Restrictions system behavior vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Screen Time parental controls cover storefront language. iOS Settings and App Store Connect actually separate restriction enforcement from product-page copy:

  • Content Restrictions hub (内容与隐私访问限制 / 内容限制) — master parental gate under Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions. Controls iTunes & App Store purchases, Allowed Apps, content ratings, Siri web search, Game Center, and privacy permission toggles. Requires a Screen Time passcode to change restrictions when enabled. Controls device behavior — does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • iTunes & App Store Purchases (购买项目) — in-app purchase, install, and delete controls for App Store and iTunes content. Listing copy may reference purchase-gate pickers when your gallery shows them.
  • Allowed Apps (允许的 App) — toggles for which built-in and third-party apps a child can access — Mail, Safari, Camera, Siri, and similar. Match frame-by-frame to what your screenshot shows.
  • Content ratings (内容分级) — age and maturity filters for movies, TV shows, apps, books, and web content inside the restrictions pane.
  • Siri web search — toggle that blocks Siri from returning web search results on managed devices. Distinct from Siri voice picker marketing on the Siri Voices listing guide.
  • Game Center — toggle that blocks multiplayer, leaderboards, and Game Center social features. Distinct from Game Center achievement listing copy when achievements are the lead story.
  • Privacy permissions — Location Services (定位服务), Contacts (通讯录), Photos (照片), Camera (相机), Microphone (麦克风), and related toggles. Listing copy should name the permission your frame shows.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of restriction support in your app marketing.
