What good zh-Hans HealthKit listing copy looks like

Listing fields should name the Health workflows you actually ship — aligned with your screenshot order — without overstating clinical value:

  • Subtitle — one concrete promise: “睡眠与 Apple 健康同步” or “跑步记录写入健康 App” — not vague “守护您的健康”
  • Keywords — mix category terms (睡眠, 跑步, 经期, 冥想) with Apple Health intent (健康, 运动) within Connect’s ~100-character zh-Hans budget; see Simplified Chinese keywords guide
  • Description — short paragraphs for each HealthKit type you read or write — mirror screenshot sequence; describe sync behavior honestly, not medical outcomes
  • Screenshot captions on Health frames — name the on-screen action: “一键同步到 Apple 健康” not “强大功能”
  • Metric label caption lines — when your gallery shows example data, caption sheet lists zh-Hans equivalents for designer re-export (步数, 心率, 睡眠分析)
  • Voice consistency — same 你/您 choice as your description across overlay lines and promotional text
