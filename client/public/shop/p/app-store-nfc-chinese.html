NFC engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Apple's NFC workflow covers storefront language. Xcode, entitlements, and Connect actually separate tag-reading behavior from product-page copy:

  • NFC capability — toggled in Xcode for apps that read or write tags. Controls whether the app can invoke Core NFC sessions. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Core NFC / NFCNDEFReaderSession / NFCTagReaderSession — Swift APIs that present the system scan sheet. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains tap-to-interact to buyers.
  • NDEF payload & tag formats — runtime parsing of URI records, text records, or custom payloads. Your listing may mention read and write; configuring tag data is engineering work, not listing copy.
  • Background tag reading & App Clip triggers — entitlement and Info.plist configuration for always-on or clip entry points. Configuring behavior is engineering — AppLocale does not touch it.
  • Privacy nutrition labels — App Store Connect privacy questionnaire when NFC touches user data. Filing accurate answers is compliance work — AppLocale does not complete it for you.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of NFC flags in the binary.

A working NFC integration and a localized China listing are both required when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other.
