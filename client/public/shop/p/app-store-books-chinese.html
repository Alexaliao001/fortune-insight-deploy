Books-specific listing copy — what translates differently

Books-app-centric listings use vocabulary general reading guides rarely cover. zh-Hans copy should reflect how Chinese App Store readers describe book workflows — not a literal paste of US marketing:

图书 vs 图书应用 vs Apple Books

US pages say “Apple Books” or “Books app.” China storefront copy often uses 图书 for the books category and reader intent, 图书应用 when naming the app category or Books home integration on gallery frames, and Apple Books when the English brand is already established on your en-US page. AppLocale aligns phrasing with each screenshot — not one blanket term for every overlay.

电子书 vs PDF vs 有声书

Reflowable ebook frames, in-book reading chrome, and EPUB-style detail pages need 电子书 — digital books you read on screen. Fixed-layout PDF reading surfaces may name PDF when the screenshot shows document-style pages. The Audiobooks tab, audiobook Now Playing, chapter lists, and Share Audiobook cards need 有声书 — listened titles, not read ones. Do not use 歌曲 or 播客 on Books frames — those belong to Music and Podcasts marketing.

书库 vs 书店 vs collections

The Library tab, saved ebooks, PDFs, and personal shelves need 书库 — your owned or saved titles. Book Store / Audiobook Store browse and purchase frames need 书店 or storefront phrasing aligned to the screenshot. Custom collections and Want to Read rows need collection vocabulary buyers recognize — not English “Library” or “Store” left on overlays. AppLocale matches the tab or badge visible on each frame — library, store, and collections are different buyer surfaces in the Books app.

阅读 vs 听书

Ebook Now Reading, page-turn / scroll chrome, highlights, notes, and Search inside a book need 阅读 — reading actions on screen. Audiobook playback, chapter scrubbing, and Spoken Content badges need 听书 — listening actions. Read Aloud on an ebook frame may combine both — captions should match whether the screenshot shows text reading or spoken playback.

Highlights, notes & Search inside a book

Margin highlights, note panels, and in-book annotation UI need concise zh-Hans — not English “Highlight” or “Note” micro-copy burned into captions. Search inside a book frames need find-in-text vocabulary aligned to the screenshot — not generic “Search anything” slogans.

Share Book / Share Audiobook cards

Share sheets and preview cards for ebooks or audiobooks need concise zh-Hans captions — 分享图书, 分享有声书 — rather than English “Share Book” or “Share Audiobook” that breaks gallery consistency.

Series, Reading Goals & reading chrome

Series landing pages and multi-volume book details need series vocabulary aligned to the frame. Reading Goals summaries need annual or streak phrasing buyers recognize. Page-turn vs scroll reading mode badges each need frame-matched zh-Hans — distinct from PDFKit engineering scope.
