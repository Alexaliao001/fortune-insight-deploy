Voicemail engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume Live Voicemail or transcription capability in the binary covers storefront language. Xcode, telephony stacks, and Connect actually separate voicemail behavior from product-page copy:

  • CallKit & CXProvider — configured in your app to register as a call provider or handle call events. Controls telephony integration depth. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • Speech-to-text pipelines — on-device or cloud transcription that converts voicemail audio to text. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains transcription value to buyers.
  • In-app voicemail UI — inbox lists, transcription result screens, playback controls, and caller-ID labels. Your listing may mention visual voicemail; implementing transcription routing is engineering work, not listing copy.

  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of Live Voicemail flags in the binary.
