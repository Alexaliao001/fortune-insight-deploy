ATT engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume ATT capability covers storefront language. Xcode, Developer portal, and Connect actually separate tracking behavior from product-page copy:

  • ATTrackingManager & IDFA access — in-app code that calls requestTrackingAuthorization and reads the advertising identifier when authorized. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • NSUserTrackingUsageDescription — Info.plist usage string shown before the system ATT sheet. In-app and binary localization, not Connect metadata.
  • Allow Apps to Request to Track — system toggle under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking that gates whether apps may show the ATT prompt. Buyer device setting — not listing copy.
  • App Privacy questionnaire & nutrition labels — structured disclosures Apple renders in Privacy Details. Separate from ATT prompt marketing on screenshots. See privacy nutrition labels listing guide — not this page.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of ATT implementation in the binary.

Working ATT in the binary and a localized China listing are both required when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other.

FAQ

What is App Tracking Transparency?

App Tracking Transparency (ATT) is Apple's iOS/iPadOS framework requiring apps to show a system permission prompt before tracking users across other companies' apps and websites for advertising. Buyers control it via Allow Apps to Request to Track under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Chinese devices the system sheet uses 跟踪 App 请求 and 允许 App 请求跟踪. Marketing that on your en-US page does not auto-fill zh-Hans Connect fields.

No. AppLocale rewrites App Store Connect metadata — name, subtitle, keywords, description, and screenshot caption lines on your zh-Hans product page. How is ATT listing copy different from Mail Privacy Protection, Private Relay, and Hide My Email?

Separate Apple privacy features. This page covers product-page marketing for the cross-app tracking permission prompt — 跟踪 App 请求, 允许 App 请求跟踪, Ask App Not to Track, Allow Tracking. Mail Privacy Protection covers hide-IP when reading email — 邮件隐私保护. Private Relay covers encrypted Safari browsing — 专用代理. Hide My Email covers relay email addresses — 隐藏电子邮件. AppLocale delivers Connect listing fields only for whichever surface your screenshots actually show.

How is ATT different from App Privacy Report and nutrition labels?

App Privacy Report is a Settings dashboard summarizing app sensor and network access — not the ATT permission prompt. Nutrition labels are structured Privacy Details disclosures on the product page. ATT listing copy markets the tracking permission sheet and pre-prompt onboarding on screenshots — a different surface from privacy report dashboards or nutrition-label badges.

How should zh-Hans listing copy talk about tracking permission without over-claiming?

Listing copy should explain why your app may request tracking permission — personalized ads, attribution, relevant offers — without implying ATT improves China rankings, without selling tracking as a feature, and without promising opt-in rates.
