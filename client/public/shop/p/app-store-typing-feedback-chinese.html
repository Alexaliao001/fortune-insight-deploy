What Apple Typing Feedback is — and what your listing may claim

Apple ships Typing Feedback under Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Typing Feedback. When enabled, the device speaks feedback as the user types on onscreen or external keyboards. Western indie listings often show one or more of these surfaces on the US product page. AppLocale covers paste-ready zh-Hans copy for whichever your screenshots and description already claim:

  • Speak Characters (字符反馈) — hear each letter spoken as you type. Optional Character Hints speak phonetic letter names (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie). Character feedback delay can be adjusted with +/- buttons so characters speak only after a pause between key presses.
  • Speak Words (朗读词语) — hear each word spoken as you finish typing it. Gallery frames may show keyboard demos with spoken word feedback badges.
  • Speak Auto-text (朗读自动文本) — hear auto-corrections and auto-capitalizations spoken as they appear above the keyboard. Screenshot overlays often show autocorrect bubbles with spoken-feedback callouts.
  • Hold to Speak Predictions (按住以朗读输入预测) — touch and hold a prediction word to hear it spoken. Requires Predictive keyboard enabled under Settings → General → Keyboards. Marketing art may show hold-to-listen prediction demos — not generic Predictive Text bars alone.
  • Spoken Content settings alignment (口述内容) — voice picker, speaking rate, and pronunciation controls under the Spoken Content branch when your English gallery shows Settings-style captures. Include 口述内容 vocabulary only when your US listing already frames Spoken Content settings — not as a generic hub paragraph.

Your gallery may lead with a speak-characters demo on one frame and hold-to-speak predictions on the next. zh-Hans caption lines should name the correct surface per frame — a character-feedback toggle is not a Speak Selection menu, and a prediction hold demo is not Dictation voice input unless that is what the screenshot shows.
