ARKit engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume AR capability covers storefront language. Xcode, entitlements, and Connect actually separate AR runtime behavior from product-page copy:

  • ARSession & world tracking — in-app code that detects planes, anchors models, and runs Measure. Controls how users place objects in space. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • RealityKit entities & USDZ assets — 3D content loaded at runtime. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains AR value to buyers.
  • NSCameraUsageDescription — Info.plist string shown in the system permission sheet when AR uses the camera. Separate from Connect listing fields.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) localization — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of ARKit integration.

Working AR experiences in the binary and a localized China listing are both required when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other. AppLocale covers how you describe AR placement and measurement on the product page in zh-Hans, not how you implement ARKit or RealityKit.
