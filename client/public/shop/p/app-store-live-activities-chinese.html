What good zh-Hans Live Activities listing copy looks like

Align tone with your en-US listing and name the surfaces buyers actually see:

  • Name & subtitle (30 chars each) — lead with the outcome (live delivery ETA, game score, workout timer) not SDK jargon
  • Keywords (100 chars) — Simplified Chinese queries for live updates, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island where relevant — not English API names stuffed into the hidden field
