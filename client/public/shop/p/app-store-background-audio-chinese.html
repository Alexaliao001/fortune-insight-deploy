Background audio engineering vs zh-Hans listing copy — two different jobs

Teams assume background playback capability in the binary covers storefront language. Xcode, AVFoundation, and Connect actually separate audio behavior from product-page copy:

  • AVAudioSession & UIBackgroundModes — configured in your app to keep audio playing when the screen locks or the user switches apps. Controls playback routing. Does not generate Chinese listing text.
  • MPNowPlayingInfoCenter & remote commands — populates lock-screen Now Playing artwork, title, and transport buttons. Separate from the zh-Hans description paragraph that explains keep-listening value to buyers.
  • In-app player UI — mini player bars, queue lists, playback speed controls, and episode titles. Your listing may mention background playback; implementing audio session categories is engineering work, not listing copy.
  • Pricing and Availability → China — makes the app downloadable on the China App Store. Separate from language rows.
  • App Store tab → Chinese (Simplified) — independent row for title, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, screenshots, and caption lines. Empty or English here means China buyers see English fallback regardless of background audio entitlements in the binary.

Working background playback in the binary and a localized China listing are both required when you sell on the China storefront — neither replaces the other.
