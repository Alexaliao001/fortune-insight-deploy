Photographic Styles vs Portrait mode vs Night Mode vs True Tone vs Camera Control — do not conflate

  • Night Mode listing copy (different) — low-light capture with longer exposure and computational blending: Night Mode, 夜间模式, 夜景模式. Low-light photography — not Rich Contrast or Vibrant tone presets at normal lighting. Covered on our Night Mode guide.
  • Cinematic Mode listing copy (different) — shallow depth-of-field video recording with focus transitions: Cinematic mode, 电影效果模式. Video capture marketing — not still-photo Photographic Styles presets.
  • True Tone display listing copy (different) — automatic display white balance matching ambient light: True Tone, 原彩显示, 原彩. Display color temperature — not Camera capture tone presets. Covered on our True Tone guide.
  • Auto Brightness listing copy (different) — screen brightness adapting to ambient light: Auto-Brightness, 自动亮度. Display brightness — not capture tone. Do not conflate with Photographic Styles.
  • Camera Control listing copy (different) — iPhone 16 physical camera button with light press, half-press, slide, and click gestures: Camera Control, 相机控制. Hardware button marketing — not tone-preset picker UI. See Camera Control guide.
  • Continuity Camera listing copy (different) — iPhone as Mac webcam, Desk View, Center Stage: Continuity Camera, 连续互通相机. Mac webcam workflows — not on-device Photographic Styles at capture. See Continuity Camera guide.
  • Subject Lift & Clean Up (different) — lift subject cutouts or erase objects from photos — 主体提取, 清理照片. Post-capture editing — not capture-time tone presets. See Subject Lift guide and Clean Up guide.
  • Visual Look Up, Live Text & Visual Intelligence (different) — identify subjects, copy text, or point-the-camera look-up — 视觉查找, 实况文本, 视觉智能. Separate Photos and Camera sub-features outside Photographic Styles scope.

Portrait mode blurs the background. Night Mode brightens low-light shots. Photographic Styles adjusts tone and color at capture. True Tone adjusts display white balance.
