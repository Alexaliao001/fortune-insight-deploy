What good zh-Hans watch complication listing copy looks like

Complication-centric listings name the wrist workflow in plain zh-Hans — not a word-for-word mirror of English brochure lines:

  • Subtitle — one clause on at-a-glance wrist data or quick launch; room for complication-adjacent search terms within 30 characters
  • Keywords — mix category terms (健身, 天气, 待办) with watch-face intent (表盘, 复杂功能); avoid English-only "complication" stuffing
  • Description — lead with the complication workflow; mention Corner or Graphic Circular slots only when your app actually supports them
  • Screenshot overlays — short lines naming what the frame shows: 添加到表盘, 角位复杂功能, 圆形表盘组件 — not "Graphic Circular complication" pasted through Google Translate
  • Voice match — same 你/您 and punctuation habits as your other zh-Hans fields

zh-Hans complication captions can differ idiomatically from en-US overlays when the on-screen feature is the same — avoid mirroring comma-heavy English marketing onto Watch art.
